## 5. Cross-site patterns applied to ExLane

**Recurring features (implemented):** diagnostic quiz as primary funnel (quiz.html); gated workbook lead magnet on every guide; segmented email capture (home, guides, quiz-stage, exit-intent); intake questionnaire → coach match (coaching.html + homepage); Start-here roadmap; press/trust rows; credential/review byline; numbered-step guides with TOC; "scripts" content; first-person stories with flair categories; pinned crisis resources; quick-exit; save-to-device tools; sticky primary CTA; related/trending rails; dedicated advertise, careers, donate and contest pages with structured forms.

**Monetization stack (implemented):** AdSense leaderboard/in-article/rect/sky slots + ads.txt; YouTube nocookie facades + playlist; coaching/therapy referral lead-gen; donation tiers (one-time, monthly, sponsor-a-prize) with spend transparency; sponsorship & native placements; creator features; contest prize sponsorship; careers/partner listing for coaches.

**Deliberately avoided:** Refinery29-level ad density; "get your ex back" manipulation products; unlabelled sponsored content; any account requirement for tools.
